# Co-ordinator

## Role

The Co-ordinator is responsible for maintaining communication between the team members and ensuring that different activities are properly coordinated.

## Functions

The main functions of the Co-ordinator are:

1. Communicate with team members.
2. Organize team meetings.
3. Maintain the event schedule.
4. Coordinate activities.
5. Share important information with the team.
6. Track task completion.
7. Help resolve communication problems.

## Communication

The Co-ordinator acts as a communication link between the different team members.
