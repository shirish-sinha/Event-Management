# Manager

## Role

The Manager is responsible for the overall planning, supervision and successful execution of the event.

## Functions

The main functions of the Manager are:

1.⁠ ⁠Plan the overall event.
2.⁠ ⁠Assign responsibilities to team members.
3.⁠ ⁠Set important deadlines.
4.⁠ ⁠Monitor the progress of the event.
5.⁠ ⁠Coordinate with the Treasurer, Co-ordinator and Designer.
6.⁠ ⁠Solve problems that occur during planning.
7.⁠ ⁠Ensure that the event is completed successfully.

## Event Planning

The Manager should create a basic event plan containing:

•⁠  ⁠Event name
•⁠  ⁠Date and time
•⁠  ⁠Venue
•⁠  ⁠Number of expected participants
•⁠  ⁠Required resources
•⁠  ⁠Budget
•⁠  ⁠Team responsibilities

## Team Management

The Manager coordinates the different roles:

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Treasurer | Budget and finances |
| Manager | Overall management |
| Co-ordinator | Communication and coordination |
| Designer | Creative and visual design |

## Responsibilities Before the Event

•⁠  ⁠Create the event plan.
•⁠  ⁠Assign tasks.
•⁠  ⁠Set deadlines.
•⁠  ⁠Monitor progress.
•⁠  ⁠Conduct team meetings.
•⁠  ⁠Coordinate with all team members.

## Responsibilities During the Event

•⁠  ⁠Supervise the event.
•⁠  ⁠Handle unexpected problems.
•⁠  ⁠Ensure that team members complete their tasks.
•⁠  ⁠Coordinate activities.

## Responsibilities After the Event

•⁠  ⁠Review the event.
•⁠  ⁠Collect feedback.
•⁠  ⁠Check the final expenses.
•⁠  ⁠Discuss what went well and what could be improved.

## Goal

The main goal of the Manager is to ensure that all parts of the event work together smoothly.
