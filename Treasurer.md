# Treasurer

## Role

The Treasurer is responsible for managing and maintaining the financial information related to the event.

## Functions

The main functions of the Treasurer are:

1. Prepare the event budget.
2. Keep track of expected expenses.
3. Record money received and spent.
4. Monitor the available budget.
5. Maintain financial records.
6. Report the financial status to the Manager.
7. Help ensure that the event stays within its budget.

## Budget Planning

The Treasurer should prepare an estimated budget before the event.

| Expense | Estimated Cost |
|---------|----------------|
| Venue | ₹10,000 |
| Food | ₹8,000 |
| Decorations | ₹3,000 |
| Marketing | ₹2,000 |
| Miscellaneous | ₹2,000 |
| **Total** | **₹25,000** |

> The values above are examples and can be changed according to the actual event.

## Financial Responsibilities

The Treasurer should:

- Maintain a record of expenses.
- Check expenses before payment.
- Keep receipts and financial records.
- Inform the Manager about major expenses.
- Prepare a final expense report after the event.

## Goal

The main goal of the Treasurer is to make sure that the event's money is planned and used responsibly.
